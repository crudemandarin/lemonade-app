# Design Session Notes

**Goal:** leave with an agreed deliverables list, clear rules, and stated assumptions. Take notes in SPEC shape.

## 0-5 min · Restate
- [ ] "Here's how I understand it: ___. What am I missing?"
- [ ] Who is the user? What does one full use look like, start to finish?

## 5-20 min · Scope (most important)
- [ ] Smallest version that feels complete?
- [ ] Must-have vs. nice-to-have?
- [ ] Anything explicitly NOT wanted?
- [ ] Narrow + polished, or broad + shallower?

## 20-32 min · Behavior & rules
- [ ] What decisions does the user make? What outcomes do they see?
- [ ] Notion of time (days/turns)? Does state carry over?
- [ ] What drives results (price, weather, inventory, demand, cost)?
- [ ] Random vs. deterministic? Specific formulas, or my call (document it)?
- [ ] Win / lose / end conditions?
- [ ] Edge cases: out of stock, zero customers, negative profit, bad input

## 32-38 min · Interface, state, quality
- [ ] Interface: CLI / web / API? Is UI polish weighed heavily?
- [ ] Single-player local, or multi-user?
- [ ] Anything persisted between runs, or in-memory is fine?
- [ ] Testing vs. features: what balance? Extensible design or keep simple?
- [ ] Docs/diagrams they want to see? Any stack constraints?

## 38-45 min · Close
- [ ] Read back the deliverables list. "Did I capture everything?"
- [ ] "Top two things you'd most want done well?"
- [ ] Walkthrough focus: decisions, abstractions, or demo?
- [ ] Slack OK for ambiguities mid-build?

## Moves
- **Propose, don't just ask:** "I'd suggest ___ because ___. Fit?"
- **"Up to you" answer:** say your default aloud, log it as an assumption.
- **Short on time:** restate, must-haves, interface, persistence.
